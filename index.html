Omelette Recipe

A simple recipe on how to make an omelette.

See more recipes on my favourite website!

Ingredients

  • 2–3 large eggs
  • 1 tablespoon butter (or oil)
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 2 tablespoons milk or water (optional, for extra fluffiness)
  • Optional fillings: shredded cheese, diced tomatoes, onions, mushrooms, or fresh herbs

Instructions

  1. Whisk the eggs: Crack the eggs into a bowl. Add your splash of milk or water, salt, and pepper. Whisk well with a fork or whisk until fully blended and slightly foamy.
  2. Heat the pan: Place a non-stick skillet over medium-low heat and add the butter. Let it melt and foam, tilting the pan so the bottom and sides are coated.
  3. Pour the eggs: Pour the egg mixture all at once into the warm pan. Let it cook undisturbed for about 20 to 30 seconds until the edges begin to set.
  4. Gently stir and lift: Use a rubber or plastic spatula to gently pull the set edges toward the center, tilting the pan to let the runny liquid flow into the empty hot spaces.
  5. Add fillings: When the top is still slightly creamy and soft ("drooly"), sprinkle your cheese or cooked vegetables evenly over one half of the omelette.
  6. Fold and serve: Gently fold the omelette in half over the filling using your spatula. Cook for another 30 seconds to 1 minute to melt the cheese, then slide it right onto a warm plate and serve.
